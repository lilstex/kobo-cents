## What we collect

Your email address and a hashed password (we never store your actual
password). The stocks you favorite and how you tag them, watching or
owned. Alert rules you create, once alerts exist. Basic analytics
events (pages viewed, features used) and error reports if something
breaks. Your IP address, briefly, for abuse prevention (rate limiting,
bot mitigation).

We do not collect your trading account details, bank details, or any
information about purchases you make elsewhere. Kobo & Cents never
executes a trade, so it never needs that information.

## Why we collect it

To create and secure your account, to remember your favorites and
preferences, to understand which parts of the product are actually
used (so we build the right things next), and to keep the service
secure against abuse.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run this product:
Mailtrap (sending you account emails), Paystack and Flutterwave (once
payments exist, processing your payment, we never see your full card
number), Sentry (error monitoring), PostHog (product analytics),
Cloudflare (security and content delivery), and Anthropic (for the
market-data research process described in our engineering
documentation). None of these providers may use your data for their
own purposes.

## Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. It's
required for the service to work and isn't optional. If you accept
analytics cookies, we also set a cookie to support that; you can
decline this and the product still works fully.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete
your account, we anonymize your personal information but may retain
payment records as required for accounting and legal purposes.

## Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or
ask us to delete your account. Contact us to exercise any of these.

## Children

This service isn't directed at children and isn't intended for use by
anyone under 18.

## International transfers

Our infrastructure may be hosted outside Nigeria. Where that's the
case, we take reasonable steps to protect your data in line with
applicable law.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address
published on the site.
