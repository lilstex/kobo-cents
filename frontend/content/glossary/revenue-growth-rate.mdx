## What it measures

Revenue growth rate is the percentage change in revenue against the
same period a year prior. Straightforward on its own, but worth
reading alongside profitability, a company can grow revenue quickly
while losing money on every additional naira or dollar of it, growth
alone isn't automatically good news.

Slowing revenue growth, even while still positive, is often the
earliest visible sign of a company's momentum changing, well before
it shows up anywhere else in the numbers.

## A worked example

A company reported revenue of 4.2 billion last year and 4.62 billion
this year. Revenue growth rate: (4,620,000,000 − 4,200,000,000) /
4,200,000,000 = 10%.

## How Kobo & Cents uses it

Revenue growth rate rounds out the valuation and growth category. A
slowing growth rate paired with an otherwise strong profitability
picture is a genuinely common pattern the explainability panel is
built to name directly, "revenue growth has slowed the last two
quarters" is exactly this metric showing up in that sentence.
